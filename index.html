Biography

I am a visiting scholar in the Computer Science Department at Stanford University, working with Prof. Maneesh Agrawala. My research interest lies in generative models, with a focus on improving the controllability of image and video generation.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Peking University, advised by Dr. Shanghang Zhang, and my B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University in 2023.
